# 💧 Venkateshwara AquaTech – RO Service Website

A modern, responsive business website developed as a **freelance project** for **Venkateshwara AquaTech**, a water purification and RO service business based in Chennai.

The website provides customers with information about RO systems, services, industrial water treatment solutions, available models, and direct contact options.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://venkateshwaraaquatect.in/

---

## 📌 About the Project

Venkateshwara AquaTech required a professional online presence to showcase their RO products and services and make it easier for customers to contact them.

I designed and developed a responsive business website focused on:

- Clear presentation of RO products
- Service information
- Industrial RO solutions
- Customer enquiry and contact options
- Mobile-friendly experience
- Professional business branding
- Easy navigation for customers

This project was developed as a **freelance client project** and has been deployed for real-world use.
